The pattern

Nobody scheduled almost any of this.

An album's first birthday. A three year old record. A countdown three days early. Five rooms in a week with nothing to promote. Fans opened every one of them.

Doing one

It takes a date and an artist handle.

  1. Confirm the channel. If fans built one already, we connect it rather than starting again.
  2. Pick a time that suits the fans, not the office, and tell the regular hosts first. They fill the room before you post anything.
  3. Announce it from the artist account. Date, time, time zone, one link. Nothing else is needed.
  4. Go live from a phone. Connect the streaming account, queue a few songs, then say hello.
  5. On air: read names out, take requests, talk between songs, and say the room stays open when you go.

One week is comfortable. Two days is possible.

What you can put in the room

Four things, all of them quick.

  • Digital purchase. The single or album, bought in the room while it plays. About a week.
  • Shopify shop. Your existing store, opened inside the room. About a week.
  • Voice note. Thirty seconds recorded on a phone, for when a live date will not work. Two days.
  • Fan Channel. The permanent room fans join once and keep between releases. A few days.

To start we need the artist handle, a date, and anything you want to sell.
