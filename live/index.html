What it does

The audience runs daily. The artist makes it spike.

Fans keep it warm every day. You bring the spike: across the shows we have measured, the day the artist joined ran between two and forty times an ordinary day on that channel.

Use it where you already have a moment. A release, an anniversary, a tour announce.

How to run one

It takes a date and an artist handle.

  1. Confirm the channel. If fans built one already, we connect it rather than starting again.
  2. Pick a time that suits the fans, not the office, and tell the regular hosts first. They fill the show before you post anything.
  3. Announce it from the artist account. Date, time, time zone, one link.
  4. Go live from a phone. Connect the streaming account, queue a few songs, say hello.
  5. On air, read names out, take requests, and say the channel stays open when you go.

One week is comfortable. Two days is possible.

What you can add

Four things, all of them quick.

  • Digital purchase. The single or album, bought during the show. About a week.
  • Shopify shop. Your existing store, opened inside the show. About a week.
  • Voice note. Thirty seconds recorded on a phone, for when a live date will not work. Two days.
  • Fan Channel. The permanent home fans join once and keep between releases. A few days.

To start we need the artist handle, a date, and anything you want to sell. We will build the show around it.
