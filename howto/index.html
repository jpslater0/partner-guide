How to run a listening party.

Everything from picking the date to walking off air.

The whole thing runs off a phone. No production, no crew, no set build. This guide is the plan, the script, and the fourteen taps.

Before anything else

What your fans need.

Every play in the room is a real stream, because each fan is playing it on their own account. That means each fan connects a paid streaming account when they arrive: Spotify Premium or Apple Music.

The week before

Four things, none of them hard.

  1. Pick the moment. Release day, a single drop, an anniversary, a tour announcement. Anything with a reason to gather.
  2. Lock the time. Post it early so you maximise fan turnout. Fans will build their day around it.
  3. Name the host. Either the artist or a trusted fan account. Both work. Decide which and tell people.
  4. Send us the assets. For a channel: your preferred font, your preferred colours, and let us know if you want Shopify, a voice note or digital music attached.

The announce

Four lines. This is the whole script.

Listening party for [record].

[Day] at [time] [timezone].

We are playing it together on Stationhead, live.

stationhead.com/[channel]

Post it from the artist account. One link, no redirects. Repost it two hours before you go on.

Going live · 1 of 6

Start the show.

Open your profile and tap Go on air. Name the show. That name is what fans see in the room and on the recording afterwards, so make it the record or the occasion.

Going live · 2 of 6

Load the music.

Two separate things here. The walk on song plays automatically the moment you go live, so the room is never silent while you find your feet. The show playlist is everything after it.

Build the playlist in the order you want it heard. You can add more once you are live, so it does not have to be final.

Going live · 3 of 6

Work the checklist.

The mic test wants a few seconds of normal speech, then silence. Speak at the volume you will actually use.

We recommend doing steps three and four. Notify followers puts the show in front of people already following you, and Share is the link you post. They are the difference between a full room and an empty one.

Going live · 4 of 6

Go on air.

A countdown, then you are in the room. Say hello before the first track. The people who showed up early are the ones who will carry the rest of the night.

Going live · 5 of 6

The three mic modes.

This is the control people miss, and it is the answer to how do I talk over the song.

  • Talk Only. Music ducks out, just your voice. Use it between tracks.
  • Talk + Music. You over the record. Use it to introduce a song.
  • Music Only. Mic off, record plays clean. Use it for everything else.

Swipe left to open the chat and read names out. Fans love it!

Going live · 6 of 6

Signing off.

Make sure to let fans know you are leaving and thank them for joining.

Save the recording. It sits on the profile and keeps earning after the room empties.

While you are on air

Five things that work.

  • Talk between tracks, not over the hook.
  • Read names out of the chat.
  • Say what the song is about before you play it.
  • Tell people to keep it playing after you leave. They will.
  • Come back on at the end for thirty seconds.

Pick one

Formats that work.

  • Release party. Play the record front to back at midnight.
  • Countdown. Fans host the hour before, artist joins for the drop.
  • Catalog night. An anniversary played in album order.
  • Drop in. Ten minutes, unannounced, on a fan-hosted show.
  • Tour warm up. The setlist, in order, the week before the run.
